Contest Mechanics & Rules

1. Scoring & Timing

Each round consists of questions with designated point values and custom countdown timers. Teams submit answers through their physical table response boards.

2. Power-Up Mechanics

  • Step-Up Transformer (Double Points): 1 card per team. Multiplies correct points earned by 2x for that chosen round.
  • Voltage Drop (Sabotage): 2 cards per team. Deducts 1 point from a targeted team. Anti-Dogpiling Cap: Max 3 total sabotage attacks received across entire game per team.
  • Surge Protector (Shield): 2 cards per team. Grants complete immunity from sabotage attacks during that round. If no one attacks, the shield is consumed.
